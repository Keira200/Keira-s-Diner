About Us

Our Story

Keira's Diner began in 2020 as a small family kitchen with a simple dream: to serve hearty, flavourful meals that bring people together. What started as a weekend passion quickly grew into one of Kerugoya's most loved dining spots.

Every dish we prepare is made from fresh, locally sourced ingredients. From our sizzling grills to our refreshing drinks, we put care into every plate. Whether you are dining in, ordering online, or booking us for catering, we promise a warm welcome and an unforgettable meal.

We are proud to be a place where families, friends, and colleagues gather to share good food and good times.
